Keystroke Monitor Demo

Everything typed on web pages is sent to 127.0.0.1:5000 and appended to log.txt by the local server. Each line contains the timestamp, the page URL and the word.

Esc stops capture. Ctrl/Alt/Meta shortcuts and IME composition are ignored. Password fields are recorded. Reload the tab after installing or reloading the extension, and type in a normal web page (not in this popup or on about: pages). A word is saved after a 2 s pause or when you press Space/Enter/Tab. Use only on a browser and pages you own or have permission to test.
